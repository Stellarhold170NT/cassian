items ([`docs/mtail/02-module-map.md`](../../docs/mtail/02-module-map.md),
item m07): it is the central state store with the highest fan-out in the
universe (8 consumers) and the lowest dependencies count among the top
items. Frozen oracle (measured on the pin, census v9):

`datum` (m09, 8 files / 667 LOC) imports nothing from the module (D=0,
grep of `mtail/internal` over its 5 non-test files = 0 hits) — self-contained.
External non-test deps of metrics are exactly 2 modules + stdlib: glog ×2

only stdlib `context`). No `testdata/`, no non-`.go` files in the 4
packages (find = 0); no upstream `vendor/` (module proxy + go.sum).
